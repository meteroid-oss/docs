---
title: "when_refused"
description: "The change cannot take effect when asked"
---

**HTTP 422**

The `when` of a change is not allowed for it, refused at `/when/type` or `/when/date`: an immediate plan downgrade or level decrease where only the period's end is, a level increase at the period's end where only now is, a date on anything but a cancellation, a cancellation dated in the past or after the subscription's end date, an immediate plan change a minimum commitment holds to the period's end.

**What to do:** Use a `when` the change allows; its `…/preview` tells which.
