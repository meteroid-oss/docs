**HTTP 403**

The credentials are valid but may not do this: a Connect access token without the scope, delegated credentials on a route they do not reach (the money routes are never theirs), or an Express organization, which keeps API v1. A feature your plan or edition does not include is [`feature_not_available`](/api/v2/errors/feature_not_available), not this.

**What to do:** Use a secret key of the tenant, or credentials granted this scope. `GET /api/v2/me` tells what the credentials may reach.
