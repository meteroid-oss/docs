---
title: Branding
---

In the **Settings > Branding** section, you can customize how your brand appears to your Customers. Branding is configured **per invoicing entity**, so each entity can have its own logo and colors. To switch to another invoicing entity, click the double arrow in the upper right.

## Logo

Upload a logo for each invoicing entity. The logo is used:

* On your **invoices**
* In the **Customer Portal** and the **checkout** page
* In the **emails** sent to your Customers

📌 The logo must be a **PNG** or **JPEG** file, with a maximum size of **1 MB**.

## Customer Portal and Checkout

You can customize the look and feel of your Customer Portal, checkout and invoice payment pages to match your brand identity:

* **Theme**: `Light` or `Dark`.
* **Roundness**: `Sharp`, `Modern` or `Rounded` corners.
* **Accent color**: the color used for highlights and primary actions (buttons, links). Pick a color from the palette or enter a custom hex code (e.g. `#4C3A82`).

An **embed preview** shows how your settings look before you save them.

## White Labelling

With white labelling, you can:

* Hide the **“Powered by Meteroid”** mention from your portals, checkout and emails
* Remove the **“Meteroid - billing automation for SaaS”** mention at the bottom of your invoices
* Serve them from **your own domain**

📌 White labelling is not enabled by default. Contact your account manager to enable it.
