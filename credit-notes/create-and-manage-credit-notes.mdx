## Create a Credit Note

Credit notes can only be created from **finalized invoices**, regardless of their payment status.
To create one, go to **Billing > Invoices**, open the relevant invoice, then click **Actions > Create Credit Note**.

## Credit Note Types

### Credit Note Scope

When creating a credit note, you can define what should be credited:

* Credit specific line(s): You can select one or several invoice lines to cancel or adjust. This is useful for partial corrections (e.g., overbilling on a specific component).
* Cancel the entire invoice: Choose this option to fully cancel the invoice. 

    💡**Note:** If you cancel the entire invoice, you can select the option “Also open a corrected draft invoice”. Meteroid will automatically create a new draft invoice replicating the original data, which can then be edited by clicking Actions > Edit Invoice. If you do not choose this option during the credit note creation, you can always go back to the fully credited invoice and click Actions > Create Corrected Invoice to generate a new draft invoice replicating the original data.
    
### Handling Credited Amounts

If the invoice is marked as Paid or Partially Paid, you will be able to choose how the credited amount is handled. 2 options can can chosen :

* **Credit to Customer Balance**, the credited amount is added to the Customer’s balance and will automatically be applied to future **recurring invoices**.

💡**Note:** This balance is not used for manually created invoices.

💡**Note:** This balance is credited even if the associated invoice's payment status is unpaid or partially paid.

* **Refund to customer**, the credit note records that the Customer must be paid back. Once the credit note is finalized, you issue the refund from it (see [Refunds](#refunds) below).

💡**Note:** Refunds are only available for paid or partially paid invoices.

## Refunds

A **Refund** credit note doesn't move money on its own: it states how much the Customer is owed. The money can be given back from the credit note, once it is finalized.

### Issue a refund

1. Go to **Billing > Credit Notes** and open the finalized refund credit note.
2. In the **Refunds** section, click **Refund**.
3. Choose how the money is given back:
   * **Through the payment provider**: the provider sends the money back on the payment method the Customer originally paid with.
   * **Record a refund made outside Meteroid**: for a bank transfer, cheque or cash refund you have already made yourself. You can add the refund date and a reference (e.g. the wire reference).
4. Enter the **amount** and, optionally, a **reason**, then click **Refund**.

💡**Note:** Partial refunds are supported: you can refund the credit note in several steps. The amount can't exceed what is left on the credit note, nor what was actually collected on the payment.

💡**Note:** The refund is made on the **most recent settled payment** of the invoice. If the invoice has no settled payment, the **Refund** button is disabled.

💡**Note:** **Through the payment provider** is only offered when the invoice was paid through a connected payment provider. Otherwise, only the offline option is available.

### Follow a refund

The **Refunds** section of the credit note shows:

* **Refundable**: what is left to refund on this credit note.
* **Refunded**: what has already been given back.
* **In flight**: online refunds sent to the provider and not confirmed yet.

Each refund is listed below, with its status. On the invoice, refunds appear in the payment list with a negative amount and a link to their credit note.

💡**Note:** Issuing a refund doesn't change the invoice's amount due or payment status: the credit note is what reduces the invoice.

💡**Note:** Refunds can also be issued from the invoice, in the payment list. See [Refunds and reversals](https://docs.meteroid.com/invoices/managing-invoices#refunds-and-reversals).

## Managing Credit Notes

All issued credit notes can be found in **Billing > Credit Notes**, where you get an overview of their amount, status, associated invoice, and Customer.

💡 **Note:** Unlike invoices, once finalized, credit notes are not automatically sent by email to Customers.

## Language, e-invoicing and tax mentions

Credit notes are rendered by the same document pipeline as invoices, so they follow the same rules:

* They are issued in the **same eight languages**, resolved the same way. See [Invoice language](https://docs.meteroid.com/invoices/managing-invoices#invoice-language).
* Where structured e-invoicing is enabled on the invoicing entity, credit notes are issued as **EN 16931** documents too, and get the same [compliance report](https://docs.meteroid.com/invoices/managing-invoices#the-compliance-report).
* **Statutory tax mentions** are generated automatically, worded for a credit note rather than an invoice — a reverse-charged credit note states that the tax is to be accounted for by the recipient *on the original invoice*. See [Legal tax mentions on documents](https://docs.meteroid.com/settings/taxes#legal-tax-mentions-on-documents).
