---
title: "Limits and budgets"
description: "Decide what happens when credit runs out, and let customers control their spend."
---

Three controls decide whether a customer may keep consuming:

| Control | Measures | Set in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **When credit runs out** | The credit a subscription can spend | Plan details, and per customer in the wallet settings |
| **Budget** | The estimated usage spend of the billing period | Customer page, **Budgets** tab, or by the customer in the Customer Portal |
| **Entitlement limit** | The usage of one metered feature | Plan entitlements, or a customer override. See [Entitlements](/entitlements/create-and-manage-entitlements) |

<Warning>
  Meteroid never rejects usage events: every event you send is recorded and billed. Denying access only changes the answer of the **access check**. Your product must call it before serving a request, and refuse the request when access is denied.
</Warning>

## When credit runs out

Each plan defines what happens once the credit a subscription can spend is used up, under **When credit runs out**:

| Rule | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoice the overage** | Access stays allowed. Usage beyond the credit is invoiced. Default for plans without credit |
| **Deny access at zero** | Access checks return denied when the balance reaches zero, until credit is added. Default for plans that grant credit or price usage in a credit unit |
| **Invoice overage up to a cap, then deny access** | Usage beyond the credit is invoiced up to a cap per billing period, then access checks return denied |

To override the plan for one customer, open the wallet settings and set **Access at zero balance** to **Always deny access** or **Never deny access**.

Access comes back on the next check once credit is added, e.g. with a top-up. Pair **Deny access at zero** with [automatic top-up](/credits/top-ups#automatic-top-up) to avoid interruptions.

## Budgets

A budget watches the estimated usage spend of the current billing period, unbilled usage included, in one currency or credit unit. It never moves money.

On the customer page, open the **Budgets** tab and add a budget:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Applies to** | All the customer's subscriptions, or one subscription |
| **Currency or credit unit**, **Amount** | The budget |
| **At the budget** | **Soft budget**: alerts only. **Hard budget**: access checks return denied once it is reached, until the next billing period or until the budget is raised |
| **Alert thresholds** | Percentages of the budget. Default: 50, 80 and 100 |

The customer is emailed at each threshold.

When budgets are enabled in the Customer Portal (see [Credit in the Customer Portal](/credits/customer-portal)), customers can set their own budget, to be alerted only or to stop their usage.

## The access check

The access check (entitlement check API) for a metered feature returns `has_access: false` with one of these reasons, evaluated in this order:

| Reason | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `LIMIT_REACHED` | A hard entitlement limit is reached |
| `BUDGET_EXCEEDED` | A hard budget is reached |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | No credit is left for this usage, and the plan or wallet denies access at zero |

Pass `subscription_id` in the check when the usage belongs to one subscription.
