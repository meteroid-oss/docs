## Create a Quote

To create a new Quote, go to **Billing > Quotes** and click **+ Create Quote.**

Steps:

1. **Choose a Customer and a Plan.**
2. **Fill in the Quote details:**
3. **Subscription Settings:** If enabled, Meteroid will automatically create a Subscription once the Quote is signed by all the required recipients (*cf*. below). For more details (*e.g* susbcription dates, billing configuration), refer to the Creating a Subscription section.

💡 **Note**: If disabled, Account Managers can always create a subscription from a specific quote by clicking Actions > convert to subscription.

4. **Recipient(s):** Choose who should receive and sign the Quote. If you select multiple recipients, the Quote will only be considered accepted once **all recipients** have signed.

Once the required fields are complete, click **Create Quote** in the upper right.

* Quotes are created in **Draft** status by default.
* To move the Quote to **Pending** status, click **Actions > Publish Quote.**

💡 **Note**: Publishing a Quote will not automatically send it to the Customer. To do so, click Actions > Send to Customer. By doing so, the Quote will be sent to the Customer using the Email (and not the Invoicing email).

If needed, you can resend the Quote to Customers by:

* Clicking **Actions > Send to Customer**
* Clicking **Actions > Generate portal link**
* Clicking on a given quote and copy-pasting the portal link from the Recipient section and sending it manually.

Once signed by all recipients, the Quote will move to the **Accepted** status.

### Use a Quote as a Template

To create a new Quote based on an existing one, open the Quote and click **Actions > Use as Template**, or click **Use as template** in the menu of the Quote in the list. This works for Quotes in any status.

A new Quote opens, pre-filled with the plan, pricing, add-ons, coupons, minimum, texts and settings of the original Quote. The Customer, recipients and dates are left empty, so you can fill them in for the new deal.

💡 **Note**: If the plan of the original Quote is no longer available for subscription, its pricing is not copied and a message tells you so.

## Signature

Meteroid provides a built-in signature feature. The recipient simply enters their name and signs in the dedicated section to validate the Quote.

💡 **Note**: Meteroid's built-in signature featrue \*\*does not have probative legal value \*\*but serves as a lightweight, easy-to-use confirmation step.

📌 **Note:** Meteroid is continuously expanding its integration library to better support your workflows. If your organization requires integration with a third-party e-signature provider that is not currently available, feel free to contact us at 👉 [**hey@meteroid.com**](https://docs.meteroid.com/integrations/general-information#)

## Manage Quotes

In **Billing > Quotes**, you’ll find an overview of all created Quotes, along with their current status (Draft, Pending, Accepted). This gives your team full visibility on deal progress and pending customer actions.

### Edit a Draft Quote

A Quote can be edited as long as it is in **Draft** status. To do so, open the Quote and click **Actions > Edit Quote**, or click **Edit** in the menu of the Quote in the list.

The Quote form opens with all the Quote's content already filled in (plan, pricing, add-ons, coupons, minimum, custom properties…). Make your changes, then click **Save Changes**.

💡 **Note**: Once a Quote is published, it can no longer be edited.
